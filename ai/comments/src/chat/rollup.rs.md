# chat/rollup.rs

Design: `ai/research/20261014T002322---chat-design.md`, Roll-up. Pure: what lines a thread has and what each is made from; summarise.rs builds them. Sizes from `20261014T200729---step-compaction-cost.md`; prompt points from `20261014T002032---chat-rollup-prior-art.md` and OptChat's "Compactions" (`/hatimthayyil/code/optchat/optchat.md`).

Rendering
- `render`: each item's `render::item_line` and a newline, the line the view prints (render.rs.md): `t.i|kind: text`, the id from the item's place (model.rs.md), the same on every branch through it, so each item renders once. The prompt asks each finding to cite the item holding its detail as `[t.i]`, so a line points where to zoom. Memory's compactor hides ids because the model copied them; here copying them is the point.
- `CUT`: tool inputs and outputs at `CLIP` = 4 KB, messages and replies at 24 KB (half the input, so no single item fills a chunk). Items stay whole in the store.

Chunking (`chunk`)
- Only for a turn whose rendering (message and steps) is over `INPUT` = 48 KB. Greedy over its steps: whole steps while they fit; a step over the budget starts a chunk and is cut before each tool call the same way; a piece still over it (a tool call with many parallel results) is a chunk alone. The message is not in a chunk: it is each chunk call's `<context>`, clipped to 4 KB.
- The cut before an over-budget step keeps chunks stable as items are appended: a chunk is final once another follows it (test `chunks_but_the_last_stay_as_items_are_appended`). `Part::chunks` is the whole cut; `Part::done` the chunks that have lines: all once the turn is closed, else all but the last, which waits until it is closed. The last chunk of a turn is keyed at most at `Turn::last` (`chunk_last`), so markers appended after an ended turn keep its key.
- Keys: chunk `0@last`. A chunk's range is fixed by the items above `last` except in one case: a fork inside an over-budget step that is itself split into pieces could give two branches different chunks ending at the same item. Claude Code forks at user messages (claude.rs.md), so the key's first writer wins and the case is left.

Lines (`Rollup`)
- `Rollup::new(tree)`: renders every item once, then for every tip (`Tree::tips`) walks its branch (`branch`): `model::turns` on the path, chunks per turn, `Branch::lines` (turn lines of the closed turns) and `nodes` over them. Lines are merged by key across branches (first wins), so a shared prefix gives one record. Subagent items are not rolled up.
- `Source`: `Chunk` (items, message as context), `Turn` (all its items), `Chunked` (message plus chunk lines: the turn line of a turn that has chunks), `Node` (two lines). `children` are the lines it needs.
- `nodes`: aligned pairs from the first closed turn of the path, level + 1, keyed by the right half's last item. A node over turn lines is level 2; position 0 is the first turn from the root, whichever session it came from.
- `words`: a line's target, its `size` over `RATIO` (10), clamped to `WORDS`..`MAX_WORDS` (50..200). `size` counts the AI's own words and `CALL` (20) per tool call in the items a line covers, summed up the tree for chunked turns and nodes; messages and tool output count nothing, so a large Read never lengthens a line. On the ai-chat thread this puts a median turn (about 350 words, 5 calls) at 50 and the largest (2,265 words, 58 calls) at 200. A node's target is about the sum of its halves', so a node joins free until the cap binds; calls merge only turns raised to 50 by the floor, and lines at the cap. Constants until the config system.
- `free`: a line whose input fits its `limit` (512 B per 50 words asked) is that input flattened, items without their ids (`bare`): no call. Ids at the head of a free line read as a line id inside a line (`216-240|216|user: …`). Turn and chunk sizes are summed before any string is built, as the compactor asks this for every pending line after each call.
- `task`: the ruler (memory's `SCALE` repeated to the target), the order with its word target (`words`, never bytes) (`Compress this turn` / `this part of a long turn` / `this long turn, given as its message and the lines of its parts` / `Merge these two adjacent lines`), `<context>` for chunks, `<input>`. Chunk lines in a `Chunked` input are labelled with their id, `t.i+n|`.

For the view: `rollup::branch(tree, tip)` cuts one branch alone, rendering only its items (`Rollup::branch` reuses the roll-up's renderings); view.rs takes its turns and chunks from it.
