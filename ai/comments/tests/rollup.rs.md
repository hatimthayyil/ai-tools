# tests/rollup.rs

The roll-up compactor against real threads in temporary projects (`Project::init`, no git), with a fake backend.

- `Script` writes a Claude Code transcript entry by entry (one item each, so an entry's index is its sequence number); `at` hangs the next entry on an earlier one (a rewind). `turn` is a prompt and a 600-byte reply (never a free line); `steps(n)` n steps of a text, a Read and a 10 KB output (about 4.2 KB rendered), so 14 steps exceed the 48 KB input.
- The fake answers each call with a 300-byte line `<verb> #first..#last` from the item numbers in its `<input>`, so a line shows what it covers and two never join free; it records every task, can sleep, fail on a predicate, and records `stop`.
- Covered: free turn and node lines with no call; every level (a closed turn's work in two chunks, its final reply left out of them, its turn line from message, work lines and reply, turn lines, nodes to level 3), each line built once, kinds, `Who`, task contents (ruler, context, part labels); word targets scaling with the AI's words and calls but not tool output, merged lines scaling and joining free, a turn line joining its work line and reply free; a second summariser and a `build` of built keys make no call and no write; branches share the lines of their common prefix and differ after the fork; an open turn gets all but its last chunk; `build` makes the keys asked and their children only, and refuses a key that is no line; `build` waits for its slow calls, stopping none, and for a lock held two seconds, then builds; a held lock makes `summarise` return `None` at once, and the log records `busy`, `built`, `end`; a failing line stays unbuilt with the node above it, and an always-failing model stops the background summariser (`OnFail::Continue`) within two waves; under `OnFail::Stop` no call starts after a failure, and the lines of the calls running beside it are kept; a project `ai.toml` read from a subdirectory, a bad boolean named with its file, and `spawn_summariser` starting nothing when switched off.
- `spawn_summariser` switched on is not tested here: it would start this test binary with `chat build …` as arguments. The smoke run (summarise.rs.md) covered it with a binary that answers those arguments.
